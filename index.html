Chapter 01 · CU Rapidamente Club · Aug 2023 – Feb 2024

Formula car chassis.

A frame that carries every component of a university race car — designed in CAD, cut on a laser, welded on a jig.

What

Chassis structure and air-intake routing for engine performance.

How

Laser-cut steel adapters join the frame; base fixtures hold every member in place while it's welded.

Result

2nd place at TSAE 2024.

Chapter 02 · CU High Altitude Research · Sep 2023 – Jun 2024

Rocket CURS III.

The payload bay and thrust structures of a 30,000 ft rocket built for the Spaceport America Cup.

What

Interior structure that carries the payload and keeps the electronics organised.

How

Simulated deformation under load to size the structure, then planned the wiring routes and assembled it.

Result

Launched — every interior part worked and held.
